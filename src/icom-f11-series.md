# Icom IC-F11 series handheld radio

Robust "professional" (no screen) VHF or UHF handheld series released in 2001, goes with good [service manual][ICOM-F11_SM] and parts compatibility across models. Programming software: [CS-F11], [CS-F11 Adj][CS-F11_Adj], cable: [OPC-478/U/UC].

I use pair of F11S VHFs, it only has 2 channels, and configured for commercial license.

## 2 to 16 channels project

I want to have:

* more channels on VHF stations, and
* handy UHF stations for hacking, 

so I ordered "a bucket-full of borker UHF stations" from hire, gonna try mixing/matching parts. Stay tuned.

## Notes

* battery’s type detection pad should be tied to ground to transmit in high power
* configuration software has advanced modes, navigate Help -> About... and type:
  - "manufacture" for unlocked channels (still figuring out)
  - "reserve" for additional adjustments
