# MedKits

## Bycicle

First aid for fractures, abrasions, cuts, punctures and tears, calluses, bites and burns; pain relieve; prepare for transportation. Should fit in always-on 4L frame-pack together with the toolchain and the lock.

* Aluminum splint
* Cold packs
* Zip ties, duck tape
* Painkillers
* Mylar blanket
* Whistle
* Emergency sugar
* Sunscreen, burn relief ??
* Repellent
* Antiseptic wipes
* Blister kit
* Trauma shears
* Non-adhesive and gauze dressings
* Steri-strips
* Tapes and adhesive bandages
* Gloves
