Running a home off the solar

Setup

  • 4x100W mono (hopefully install 5-7 in total)
  • 100V 50A MPPT charge controller
  • 190Ah 12V AGM batteries (deep and high-current in parallel)
  • various measurement and safety devices
  • planned grid, DC-DC, and wind chargers
  • 2KW invertor, 20A 5V converter, 7A USB-C PD

It's interesing to estimate what could be done to reduce the dependency on LPG and a grid in a city flat. With the predicted rise of the energy proces, it'll be neccesary to power some vital devices, like

  • highly-efficient rice and a pressure cooker, a microwave,
  • kotatsu and IR heater.

Ability to have a backup and a tool to offload the usage seems neccesary and doable.
