Icom IC-F11 series handheld radio

Robust "professional" (no screen) VHF or UHF handheld series released in 2001, goes with good [service manual][ICOM-F11_SM] and parts compatibility across models. Programming software: [CS-F11], [CS-F11 Adj][CS-F11_Adj], cable: [OPC-478/U/UC].

I use pair of F11S VHFs, it only has 2 channels, and configured for commercial license.
